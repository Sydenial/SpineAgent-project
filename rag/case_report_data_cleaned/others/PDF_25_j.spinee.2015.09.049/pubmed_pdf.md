# Congenital Odontoid Process Separation with Atlantoaxial Dislocation Associated with Klippel-Feil Syndrome and Cervical Angular Kyphosis Secondary to C6 Wedging Vertebra

A 14-year-old boy presented with a 1-month history of a bulge on the back of the neck, without pain or neurologic symptoms. X-ray and computed tomography scans showed a complete dislocation of the atlas with respect to the axis and a separated odontoid process with a posterior fusion of C2–C3 and wedge deformity with kyphosis at C6. Magnetic resonance imaging suggested a narrowed spinal canal at the level of atlas-axis and C6. Diagnoses were congenital odontoid process separation with atlantoaxial dislocation associated with Klippel-Feil syndrome and cervical kyphosis. A C1–C2 reduction with decompression and a C6 hemivertebra reaction with anterior fusion was performed.
