# Lumbar Paget's Disease with Spinal Stenosis and Conus Medullaris Compression

## Case Report

A 51-year-old man presented with low back pain. Lumbar spine plain radiographs showed loss of height in the L1 and L2 vertebral bodies with retropulsion, but expansion in transverse and anterior–posterior dimensions, and sclerosis. T1- and T2-weighted MR images demonstrated low signal intensity in these vertebrae with some preserved intramedullary fat. Severe spinal stenosis and conus medullaris compression were also noted. No soft tissue component was identified. Postcontrast imaging revealed heterogeneous enhancement at L1 and L2, sparing the intervertebral disc spaces. The preoperative radiological diagnosis was Paget's disease. Decompression surgery and biopsy were performed, and pathology confirmed the diagnosis.

## Discussion

Paget's disease with consecutive multilevel vertebral involvement and spinal stenosis causing conus medullaris compression is rare and may be challenging. The main differential diagnoses include metastatic neoplasm, hemangioma, fibrous dysplasia, and renal osteodystrophy or primary hyperparathyroidism [1,2].
