# IMAGES OF SPINE CaRE 

## Multiple neurofibromas occupying all the neural foramens

Cervical, thoracic, and lumbar magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scans on a 43-year-old man with a history of neurofibromatosis type I revealed neurofibromas occupying all of the neural foramens (Figs. 1-5).

### Figure 1
Axial T2-weighted magnetic resonance images at the levels of the thoracic and lumbar regions showed similar findings at all other levels in the spine. The white arrowhead indicates the iso-heterogenous intensity neurofibromas passing through bilateral neural foramens.

### Figure 2
Axial T2-weighted magnetic resonance images at the levels of the thoracic and lumbar regions showed similar findings at all other levels in the spine. The white arrowhead indicates the iso-heterogenous intensity neurofibromas passing through bilateral neural foramens.

### Figure 3
Sagittal T2-weighted magnetic resonance images at the cervical, thoracic, and lumbar levels revealed similar findings at the opposite side. The white and black arrowheads indicate the iso-heterogenous intensity neurofibromas passing through all cervical neural foramens.

### Figure 4
Sagittal T2-weighted magnetic resonance images at the cervical, thoracic, and lumbar levels revealed similar findings at the opposite side. The white and black arrowheads indicate the iso-heterogenous intensity neurofibromas passing through all cervical neural foramens.
