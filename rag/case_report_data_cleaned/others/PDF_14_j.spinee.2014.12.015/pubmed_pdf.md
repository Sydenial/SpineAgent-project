# Diffuse idiopathic skeletal hyperosteosis leading to snoring and dysphagia

## Case Report

A 45-year-old man was referred to our clinic with snoring and dysphagia. Endoscopic examination was performed, which revealed a protrusion located at the posterior wall of the hypopharynx (Fig. 1). To elucidate the origin of the mass, computed tomography was performed. On both axial and sagittal planes, computed tomography revealed hyperosteosis at the cervical vertebrae causing bulging of the posterior hypopharyngeal wall (Figs. 2 and 3). The patient was diagnosed with diffuse idiopathic skeletal hyperosteosis.
