# Entirely Ossified Subdural Meningioma in Thoracic Vertebral Canal

## Case Report

A 90-year-old man presented with back pain and paraplegia one week after a fall. Magnetic resonance imaging revealed a vertebral compressive fracture of T10 as well as a subdural tumor at the T10–T11 level. The tumor was hypointense on both T1- and T2-weighted magnetic resonance images and was confirmed on computed tomography to be ossified.

Pedicle screw instrumentation and laminectomy were performed, and the subdural tumor was removed. Histological examination demonstrated that the tumor was a meningioma.
