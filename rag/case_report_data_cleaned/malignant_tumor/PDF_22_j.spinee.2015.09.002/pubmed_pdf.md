# Spinal Cord Metastasis of Lung Neuroendocrine Tumor: MR Imaging Findings

## Case Report

A 50-year-old female patient was admitted to our hospital with paralysis. She was known to have a lung neuroendocrine tumor (NET) and had previously undergone chemo-radiotherapy for NET. To further evaluate her condition, a whole-spine MRI was performed. MR images showed multiple hyperintense lesions on T2-weighted sequences at different levels of the cervical and thoracic spinal cord (Figure 1a, 2a). There was also avid enhancement of the lesions on postcontrast T1-weighted images (Figure 1b, c and 2b).
