# Unusual Presentation of Gastric Carcinoma: Back Pain

## Case Report

A 56-year-old man presented to the neurosurgery clinic with back pain. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) revealed an expansive mass involving the T5 and T6 vertebrae, with expansion of the vertebral bodies and destruction of the T6 corpus. The mass appeared hypointense on both T1-weighted (T1W) and T2-weighted (T2W) images and showed heterogeneous contrast enhancement. A pathological fracture of the T6 vertebral corpus was also observed.

Radiologic evaluation suggested that the lesion represented a metastatic mass. Following systemic investigation, the patient was diagnosed with gastric carcinoma based on endoscopic biopsy findings.
