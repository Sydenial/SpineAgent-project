# Clear Cell Ependymoma with Late Leptomeningeal and Supratentorial Metastases

## Case Report

A 32-year-old male was admitted to our department with the complaint of upper back pain for three weeks. Neurological examination was normal. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) was performed and demonstrated an intradural intramedullary mass with marked enhancement between the T1 and T3 vertebral levels. Surgery was performed, and the diagnosis was clear cell ependymoma.

Two years after surgery, a follow-up MRI was obtained. Spinal MRI demonstrated a residual mass with arachnoidal thickening and nodular arachnoidal and pial enhancement along the cervicothoracolumbar spine. Brain MRI showed multiple nodular, well-enhancing lesions consistent with metastases. The patient was diagnosed with clear cell ependymoma with late leptomeningeal and supratentorial metastases and was scheduled for radiotherapy. MRI is crucial for diagnosis and follow-up in this variant.
