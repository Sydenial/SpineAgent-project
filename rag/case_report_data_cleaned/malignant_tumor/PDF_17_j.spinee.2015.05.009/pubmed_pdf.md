# Thoracic Paraspinal Desmoid Type Fibromatosis

## Case Report

A 53-year-old female was admitted to our hospital with complaints of lower back pain and a nonmobile, palpable mass in the middle of the posterior low thoracic region. The patient’s history revealed a 12-month history of a slowly growing mass.

Magnetic resonance imaging of the thoracic spine demonstrated a well-defined mass between the T9 and L1 vertebral levels, originating from the trapezius muscle in the neighborhood of the thoracic spinous processes. The lesion was heterogeneous hyperintense on T2-weighted images and hypointense on T1-weighted images. On fat-suppressed T2-weighted images, the lesion showed heterogeneous hyperintense signal intensity. After gadolinium administration, heterogeneous strong enhancement of the mass was observed.

A fine-needle aspiration biopsy was performed, and pathologic examination diagnosed the tumor as desmoid-type fibromatosis. After surgical treatment, the patient was relieved of lower back pain. Desmoid-type fibromatosis is a benign fibroblastic tumor, and the primary treatment is wide resection with negative surgical margins [1]. Imaging plays a vital role in diagnosis and presurgical planning.
