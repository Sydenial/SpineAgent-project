# Traumatic Burst Fracture and Dislocation of the Lumbar Spine with an Intact Neurologic Function

## Case Report

A 51-year-old man presented with low back pain and limitation of movement of the lumbar spine after a fall accident. The patient had no neurologic deficit. Plain radiograph and computed tomography demonstrated a burst fracture and dislocation of the L4 vertebral body (Fig. 1). Magnetic resonance imaging showed disruption of the posterior ligamentous complex (Fig. 1).

### Management

Posterior decompression, reduction, and fixation were undertaken. Autogenous iliac bone grafting through the posterior edge break of the L4 vertebral body was performed with the help of a modified bone graft funnel (Fig. 2).

### Outcome

A satisfactory reduction of the dislocation was obtained, and a computed tomography scan confirmed the cleft of the L4 vertebral body filled with autogenous iliac bone (Fig. 3).
