# Unusual Presentation of Gastric Carcinoma: Back Pain

## Case Report

A 56-year-old man presented to the neurosurgery clinic with back pain. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) revealed an expansive mass in the T5 and T6 vertebrae, causing expansion of the vertebral bodies and destruction of the T6 vertebral corpus (Figure 1). The mass appeared hypointense on both T1-weighted and T2-weighted images and showed heterogeneous contrast enhancement. A pathological fracture was also observed in the T6 vertebral corpus (Figure 1). Radiological evaluation suggested the lesion to be a metastatic mass. Following systemic investigation, the patient was diagnosed with gastric carcinoma by endoscopic biopsy.
