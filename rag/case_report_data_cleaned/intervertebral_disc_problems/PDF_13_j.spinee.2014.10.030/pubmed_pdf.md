# IMAGES OF SPINE CARE

## Discography with epidural contrast extravasation along an exiting nerve root

A 46-year-old woman with a history of L3–L4 microdiscectomy presented for lumbar discography. During injection of the L3–L4 level, contrast extravasation was noted into the posterior epidural space and along the course of the exiting right L4 nerve root, consistent with a Grade 5 annular tear.
