# Spinal intramedullary metastasis as the first manifestation of lung cancer

## Case Report

A 60-year-old woman was admitted with back pain, lower limb sensory disturbance, and acute-onset, rapidly progressive urinary difficulties. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the thoracic spine revealed an intramedullary spinal cord lesion at the T11-12 level with contrast enhancement and edema extending above and below the lesion. Incidentally, a large mass in the upper lobe of the right lung was seen on thoracic MRI. No disease was identified in other organs on further radiological evaluation. Transbronchial needle aspiration confirmed lung adenocarcinoma.
