# IMAGES OF SPINE CARE

## Traumatic Burst Fracture and Dislocation of the Lumbar Spine with Intact Neurologic Function

A 51-year-old man presented with low back pain and limitation of movement of the lumbar spine after a fall accident. The patient had no neurologic deficit. Plain radiograph and computed tomography demonstrated burst fracture and dislocation of the L4 vertebral body (Fig. 1). Magnetic resonance imaging showed disruption of the posterior ligamentous complex (Fig. 1).

The posterior depression, reduction, and fixation surgery were undertaken. The autogenous iliac bone grafting through the posterior edge break of the L4 vertebral body was performed with the help of a modified bone graft funnel (Fig. 2).

A satisfactory reduction of the dislocation was obtained, and a computed tomography scan confirmed the cleft of the L4 vertebral body filled with autogenous iliac bone (Fig. 3).
